# Singalong glossary

**English** · [Português (Brasil)](GLOSSARY.pt-BR.md)

Singalong is a karaoke for a group in one place: everyone queues songs from their phone, a TV plays them, and the songs come from YouTube videos turned into karaoke tracks. These are the words the product uses; the on-screen Portuguese term is in parentheses.

## The party

**Room** (Sala):
The place a group sings together, identified by a short code and joined by scanning the TV's QR code.
_Avoid_: Party, lobby, session

**Room code** (código da sala):
The four-character code that identifies a Room and that guests type or scan to join.
_Avoid_: PIN, invite code

**Host** (anfitrião):
The person who controls a Room: the order of the Queue, skipping, pausing and the Room's settings.
_Avoid_: Admin, DJ, owner

**Guest** (convidado):
Anyone in a Room who is not the Host; a Guest adds songs and sings.
_Avoid_: User, player, member

**Singer** (cantor):
Whoever sings a given song: the Guest who added it and, in a Duet, the invited partner.
_Avoid_: Performer, player

**TV**:
The screen that plays the songs in a Room, shows the lyrics and the Queue, and owns the sound.
_Avoid_: Player, display, screen

**Stage** (palco):
The singer's own phone screen during their song, with the lyrics in time with the TV.
_Avoid_: Karaoke mode, singer view

**Queue** (fila):
The ordered list of songs waiting to be sung in a Room.
_Avoid_: Playlist, setlist

**Turn** (vez):
A song's moment to be sung; "your turn" means your song is next on the TV.
_Avoid_: Slot

**Fair rotation** (rodízio justo):
A Room setting that keeps the same person from singing twice in a row while someone else is waiting.
_Avoid_: Round robin, shuffle

**Yield the turn** (ceder a vez):
A Singer moving their own song one place back so the person behind sings first.
_Avoid_: Skip, postpone

**Duet** (dueto):
A song sung by two people: the one who added it and a partner they invited, who must accept.
_Avoid_: Pair, feat

**Part** (parte):
Who sings a line in a Duet: the first voice, the second voice, or both together.
_Avoid_: Role, track

**Intermission** (intervalo entre músicas):
The pause between two songs, made of the Applause and Taking the stage; the Host can turn it off or skip it.
_Avoid_: Break, interval, transition

**Applause** (aplausos):
The first half of the Intermission: the TV celebrates whoever just sang, with their Score when scoring is on.
_Avoid_: Ovation, outro

**Taking the stage** (subida ao palco):
The second half of the Intermission: the next Singer walks up to the mic on the TV while a countdown runs.
_Avoid_: Intro, loading

**Reaction** (reação):
A quick emoji a Guest sends from their phone that floats up on the TV.
_Avoid_: Like, emote

## Singing

**Key** (tom):
How high or low the instrumental plays for a song in the Queue, chosen per song with "Lower" and "Higher".
_Avoid_: Pitch, transpose, semitones

**Score** (pontuação):
A grade from 0 to 100 for how close the Singer stayed to the original melody, heard by the TV's microphone; in beta.
_Avoid_: Points, rating

**Melody guide** (guia de melodia):
Bars on the TV showing the original melody's notes, with the Singer's pitch drawn over them; in beta.
_Avoid_: Pitch graph, tuner

**Voice in the speakers** (voz nas caixas):
The TV's microphone played through the speakers together with the song.
_Avoid_: Monitor, mic passthrough

**Backing vocals** (vozes de apoio):
The original song's supporting voices, which a Singer can bring back at a chosen level.
_Avoid_: Choir, harmonies

**Original voice guide** (voz original, guia):
The original lead voice, which a Singer can bring back softly to sing along with.
_Avoid_: Vocal track, reference voice

## Songs

**Song** (música):
A YouTube video turned into a karaoke track: the Instrumental, the Lyrics and what was learned about it.
_Avoid_: Track, video, item

**Jukebox**:
The collection of Songs that are already prepared, which go into the Queue instantly.
_Avoid_: Library, catalog, processed songs

**Instrumental**:
The Song with the lead voice removed; what the TV plays.
_Avoid_: Backing track, karaoke track

**Lyrics** (letra):
The words of a Song with the time each line, and often each word, is sung.
_Avoid_: Subtitles, captions

**Lyrics source** (fonte da letra):
Where a Song's Lyrics came from: the video's captions, a lyrics search on the internet, text the Guest pasted, or none.
_Avoid_: Provider

**Preparing** (preparando):
The time between adding a new Song and it being ready to sing, while its voice is separated and its Lyrics are found.
_Avoid_: Processing, rendering, building

**Needs lyrics** (precisa de letra):
A Song whose sound is ready but whose Lyrics were not found; the Guest who added it chooses another Lyrics source.
_Avoid_: Failed, missing subtitles

**Export** (exportar):
Downloading a Song as an MP4 video, with the lyrics, to sing offline.
_Avoid_: Render, download

**Import** (importar):
The Owner adding an audio file of their own as a Song, instead of a YouTube video.
_Avoid_: Upload

## The owner's computer

**Owner** (dono):
The person who runs Singalong on their own computer, prepares new Songs and starts Sessions.
_Avoid_: Admin, Host

**Installable app** (app):
The Owner's desktop application, which prepares Songs on their computer and shows the TV and the Host page.
_Avoid_: Client, launcher

**Session** (sessão):
An Owner's working period in the Installable app; the Rooms created in it send their new Songs to that Owner's computer.
_Avoid_: Login, account

**Agent** (agente):
The part of the Installable app that takes Song requests from the server and prepares them on the Owner's computer.
_Avoid_: Bot, daemon, runner

**Engine** (motor):
The set of tools the Installable app assembles on the Owner's computer to prepare Songs.
_Avoid_: Runtime, backend

**Pairing** (pareamento):
Linking an Installable app to a Singalong server with a one-time code.
_Avoid_: Login, registration

**Local media** (mídia local):
Songs that live on the Owner's computer and reach the TV straight from it, without passing through the cloud.
_Avoid_: Offline mode, local cache

**Hybrid mode** (modo híbrido):
An Owner's Songs also copied to the cloud, so other Rooms can play them without preparing them again.
_Avoid_: Sync, backup
